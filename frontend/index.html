Student hackathon prototype. Built for the UBS Student Mini-Hackathon (Scenario 2). Not a UBS system, and not affiliated with or endorsed by UBS. Vendors are real companies, but signals marked ILLUSTRATIVE are synthetic examples written for the demo, and the UBS link field records only what is publicly stated about a relationship. Nothing here is a UBS assessment of a supplier.
